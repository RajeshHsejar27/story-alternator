# 🌌 Story Alternator — Classic Ancient Glassmorphic & Stardust Roadmap

> **Design System Aesthetics**: Literary Ancient Parchment canvas (`#fdfbf7`), Deep Midnight Cosmic Gradients (`radial-gradient(#1e1b4b, #0f172a, #020617)`), Frosted Glassmorphic Cards with golden borders, Ambient Twinkling Stardust Particle overlays, **Ubuntu Typography**, and Floating 3D Action Buttons.

---

## 🎨 1. Literary Ancient & Glassmorphic UI/UX Stack

Transitioning to **Next.js 15 (App Router, React 19)** + **Ubuntu Typography** + **Glassmorphic Glass UI**:

### 🕸️ A. Interactive Stardust Decision Tree (`React Flow`)
- **Cosmic Flowchart Canvas**: Render an interactive node-graph decision tree overlaid on a midnight stardust canvas.
- **Golden Pivot Nodes**: Canonical story nodes styled in frosted glass cards with golden borders (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`), allowing readers to click and branch narrative paths seamlessly.

### 📖 B. Ancient Parchment Dual-Reader & Diff Highlighting
- **Side-by-Side Reading Canvas**: Original text on the left panel and AI-generated alternate timeline on the right.
- **Ubuntu & Playfair Display Typography**: Body text rendered in clean **Ubuntu** font with classic drop-cap chapter starters and gold-accented dividers (`✦ ✦ ✦`).

### 🔘 C. Floating 3D Action Buttons
- Elevated floating action buttons (`transform: translateY(-3px)`) with glowing purple/amber gradient box shadows (`box-shadow: 0 10px 25px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.4)`).

## 🎧 2. Multimodal Ancient Media Enhancements

### 🎙️ A. Theatrical Voice Narration (ElevenLabs / Google Cloud TTS)
- **Period-Authentic Character Voices**: Assign unique AI voices with Regency or Gothic accents to read alternate chapter pages aloud.
- **Glassmorphic Audio Player**: Built-in floating player with sentence-by-sentence text syncing.

### 🎵 B. Ambient Scene Soundscapes
- Contextual background audio tailored to scene mood (e.g., crackling fireplace, stormy thunder, ballroom waltzes).

### 🎨 C. Graphic Novel & Comic Panel Mode
- Multi-panel graphic novel strips per alternate chapter using FLUX.1 or Stable Diffusion ControlNet for character consistency.

## 🧠 3. Advanced AI Knowledge Graph & Character Memory

### 🕸️ A. Character Relationship Graph (Neo4j / Vector RAG)
- **Dynamic Relationship Scores**: Track character emotional states and secrets across plot pivots (e.g., tracking Darcy's sentiment score toward Elizabeth from -40 to +20).

### 👤 B. Multi-POV Perspective Engine
- Rewrite scenes from different characters' viewpoints (e.g., Chapter 34 from Mr. Darcy's inner monologue vs. Colonel Fitzwilliam's perspective).
